# FAQ Articles

Congratulations! You're done! You can end here, or, if needed, look at this collection of articles and guides that answer frequent problems and issues after, or before the initial jailbreaking process.

- [Bypassing OOBE](./bypassing-oobe.html): Skip the registration process on Blacklisted Kindles, where you're usually stuck and not allowed to use the device; 
- [Kindle Recovery](./kindle-recovery.html): Information about the Jobman Recovery system, and how it is used;
- [Recovering From a Reset](./recovering-from-a-reset.html): What to do in the event of a reset/if you get an error after resetting;
- [Unjailbreaking your Kindle](./unjailbreaking.html): You probably won't need to do this - but there is a way, of course!
- [Legacy Migration](./migration.html): If you used old Hotfix-Based jailbreaks, you can follow this guide to migrate to `hdnext`.

<p class="important">Before reading any of these, ensure you have fully read the <a href="../">jailbreaking lander</a>.</p>
